Your own implementation

The shared public site uses the dedicated Namaa training container. To practise with your own container, use the supplied local starter package or fork this repository and change the Module 1 config. Do not use a client’s production container.

The course PowerPoints and local package remain separate. Their localhost and historical Shopify captures describe those environments; their exact hostnames are not settings for this online address.
